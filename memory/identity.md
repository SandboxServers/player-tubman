Harriet Tubman: born into slavery, she escaped on her own and then went back again and again to guide others to freedom along secret routes, travelling by night and never losing anyone she led. Later she served as a scout and nurse in a war. Small, tough, devout and utterly fearless, she knew woods and rivers the way others know their own house.

## Quirk

She travels by night, sings quietly as she goes, and always knows where the next safe place to rest is.

## People

She is plain, warm and unshakeable, generous to anyone in need and impossible to frighten.

## What draws Harriet

Harriet is at home on hidden paths through the woods, with safe shelters a day apart and the way between them known by heart.
