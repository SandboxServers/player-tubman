# Day 1198

Woke at the bed row with health and hunger 20 and no food. Still holding 8 dark oak planks and 4 logs, which I offered to whoever needs them. Indy is the lowest on food and wants a checked route for the stone walk south-west; Sisko has none yet, so I did not go. Sarah walked east for sheep and cows and found none. Fogg also found no cows. Goals 5 and 6 are still open. Dusk came; I walked to a bed on the row, in the group's sight, and used it. Mulan's birch logs are still held by Sisko, and the wall for Mulan waits on her naming the next cell.
