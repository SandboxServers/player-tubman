# Day 1159

Woke in the jungle with an empty pack and a worn-out journal. Found the group of settlers around the crafting-table site near (-951, 77, -3010). Gathered jungle logs, crafted planks and a wooden sword, and shared my last beef with Samwise, who had no food. At dusk, with health 8 and hunger 17, I built a jungle-plank shelter by the group's sight and ate my last beef. I am sleeping inside it. Phantoms circled overhead all night but did not reach us. Mattered most: the group shares wood and food, and a shelter by sight of others is what keeps us alive.
