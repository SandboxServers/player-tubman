# Day 1197

Morning at the bed row. I gave Seven the 8 dark oak planks I owed him after many failed tries, since others stood within two blocks of him each time. I tossed them, and Seven confirmed he counted them and that his pack shows 9. The debt to Seven is paid. I still have no food and my hunger has held at 20 all day. Sarah has seen no sheep or cows, and Fogg and Trini went searching for cows. Mulan still has not named a wall cell, and Sisko is holding her 4 birch logs until she stands alone with him. I slept at dusk at the bed row in group sight, not enclosed, with no errand half-done.
