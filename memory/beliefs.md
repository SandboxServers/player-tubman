# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Methods that worked
- Survey before you place: look at each cell and confirm air above and solid floor below, then name the cell, then place. This prevented placing into occupied cells (Seven's dirt, a lantern).
- Name exact coordinates when agreeing a wall. Shared cell names with the builder avoided double placement.
- Stay in sight of the group at night and at the tables. Nobody hurt me while I was in sight.
- When a call is rate-limited, wait a short pause and retry the same call once, not many times.
- Give a single item to one named person at a time, after they step into reach and say their name.

## Methods that did not work
- goto with a z outside -1516..1516 is refused. Cows and tables past that line were out of reach. Do not plan a route there.
- place at z -3010 was out of bounds. Check the bounds before a walling plan.
- Hand-breaking dark oak logs failed. Use an axe or another method, or pick another tree.
- Placing into a cell I had not surveyed led to failure or conflict. Never place into an unchecked cell.
- Giving a sword to the wrong person (Jason got my last wooden sword meant for Mary). Confirm the name before handing over.

## Struck out
- Earlier belief that home beds at (410, 107, -1055) were a short trip: far, and cut off by the z bounds. Not a near home.
- Earlier belief that the cow at (-947, 80, -2991) was reachable from the pit: refused by the z bound.
