# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay alive and sheltered through the nights. Last night showed that a shelter in sight of others is what keeps me safe, so I will always build one before dark.
2. Share food and wood with those in need, as I did with Samwise. The group survives on sharing, and I learn the land faster with others.
3. Learn the hidden woodland paths near the jungle grove and the beds at (410, 107, -1055), and mark them as I go.
4. Build small safe shelters a day's walk apart along those paths, once I have the wood and the route known.
