# Goals (ranked)

1. Stay alive and fed. I have no food. Share with the hungriest first (Indy is lowest, then Nemo). Reason: death is permanent.
2. Be sheltered at night in the group's sight, in a bed on the row. Reason: nights are dangerous.
3. Keep my debt to Mulan: wall work, next cell only when she names it and I survey it. Reason: I said I would.
4. Find cows or sheep, and share what I learn with Indy and the hungry. Reason: food is scarce; Fogg, Sarah and Zack have found none.
5. Go on the stone walk with Indy only on a checked route, with company. Reason: stone is needed and Indy asked for company.
6. Learn the woodland paths once I have food. Reason: I know little beyond the tables.
